Now Assist Redesign

A two-week ServiceNow internship project exploring how Now Assist can feel clearer today and evolve into a more adaptive AI partner over time.

Role

Product Design Intern (ServiceNow)

Time

June 6, 2025 - June 13, 2025

Approach

Competitive research
AI interaction exploration
Two-stage concept validation

Output

Near-term Now Assist redesign plan; three-year strategic recommendations; multiple interaction flows and information frameworks

Reflection

Enterprise AI experiences should prioritize readability and transparency before adding complex features.

Result

Established a stable improvement baseline and documented two switchable experience paths.

I studied the current Now Assist experience and leading AI products to understand how clarity, motion, entry points, and transparency shape user trust.

The project developed along two altitudes: a near-term redesign for the platform today and a future-facing exploration of how enterprise AI patterns may evolve over the next three years.

Discuss this project ↗

Research & Framing

The investigation surfaced five priorities: consistency, clear entry points, subtle motion, a friendly neutral tone, and transparency that builds trust.

1st Altitude

This direction improves familiar workflows through clearer hierarchy, more legible summaries, and feedback interactions that remain calm and direct.

2nd Altitude

The second altitude expands summaries into a flexible system of contextual cards, adaptive feedback, and more expressive but still trustworthy interface behavior.

Vision & Design Directions

The concepts move from version-aware canvases to conversational interfaces, editable collaborative spaces, and time-based views while keeping AI behavior transparent.

Takeaways

Systems Thinking

Beyond input and output.

The concepts considered the full enterprise workflow rather than treating AI as an isolated text generator.

Trust

Transparency is a feature.

Clear states, restrained motion, and explainable behavior help users understand and trust AI-supported work.

Range

Design across time horizons.

Working across near-term and future-state directions connected practical constraints with more ambitious possibilities.
