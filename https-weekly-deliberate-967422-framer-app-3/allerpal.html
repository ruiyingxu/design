Designing a calmer way for children to communicate allergies.

Category

Team

Year

AllerPal is a digital companion designed for children with food allergies and the adults supporting them. The goal was to create a visual system that feels warm, legible, and easy to act on in stressful dining situations.

From the first interaction to real-world use, the project balances playful branding with clear communication, making safety information more accessible for both kids and parents.

A friendlier, more reassuring dining experience
